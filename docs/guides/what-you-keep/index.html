What do you keep after the wipe?

Before Season 2, players expected that progression and unlocks would reset on October 15 and that the wipe would give Gold Bars. One player summed up the trade: "the wipe will only reward with gold bars to get skins.. I don't need skins" (Steam review, October 1, 2026). reported by a player, not official

Season 2 went live on October 15, 2026. The official Season 2 patch notes we read do not mention a general wipe; the only reset they mention is for players caught abusing cash and XP exploits. Check what you kept in game.

Press summaries said your cash would convert into Gold Bars and that Gold Bars and cosmetics are kept. We have not checked this against an official source, and we found no published conversion rate.

The details, with sources, are on the wipe rules page.
