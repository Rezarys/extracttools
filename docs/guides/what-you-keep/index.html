What do you keep after the wipe?

Players report that progression and unlocks reset on October 15 and that the wipe gives Gold Bars. One player sums up the trade: "the wipe will only reward with gold bars to get skins.. I don't need skins" (Steam review, October 1, 2026). reported by a player, not official

Press summaries add that your cash converts into Gold Bars and that Gold Bars and cosmetics are kept. We have not checked this against the official announcement, and we found no published conversion rate.

The details, with sources, are on the wipe rules page.
