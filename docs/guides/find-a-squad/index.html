How do I find a squad to play with?

Solo players write about it: "As someone who has 0 friends to play with in europe I can give honest solo player experience review. [...] 1/10 matches click" (Steam review, September 18, 2026).

The squad board lets you gather players outside the game: start a board, write your region, when you play and your style, and post the link where your group talks. Each player who joins posts back the new link. No account, and nothing is stored on a server.

Put only your in-game name on a board, never personal details: anyone with the link can read it.
