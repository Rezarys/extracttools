When is the WARDOGS wipe?

No next wipe date had been announced when this page was updated on October 15, 2026. Season 2 went live that day, according to the developer's announcements on the game's Steam news page.

Before Season 2, players expected a wipe on October 15: "they're going to wipe on October 15th" (Steam review, October 1, 2026) and "forced wipes are going to be implemented from October 15th" (Steam review, September 30, 2026). The official Season 2 patch notes we read do not mention a general wipe, so we cannot confirm one happened.
