How many hours does it take to level a class?

What players report, in their own words. reported by players, not official

  • Pilot to level 20: "I have played for 83 hours, spending at least 3/4 of those hours dedicated to piloting. And I have only now managed to get my pilot XP to level 20" (Steam review, October 2, 2026). That is roughly 60 hours of piloting.
  • Recon toward the .50 cal: "a little over half way there after 40 hours of pure recon gameplay" (Steam review, October 1, 2026).
  • One class: "it takes forever to get anywhere in this game without dumping 20 hours into one class alone" (Steam review, October 2, 2026).

Your pace will differ. The calculator takes your own hours and progress and tells you when you get there.
