How much XP do you get per match?

One player estimates about 5,000 XP per match: "Estimated at 5000xp per round, you will only get to level 35 from level 34 in 15 rounds" (Steam review, October 2, 2026). reported by a player, not official

Your number depends on your class and what you do in the match. To measure yours: note your XP before a match and after it, over three matches, and take the average.

Then put it in the calculator to see how long your next level takes.
