Can I finish my unlock before the wipe?

It is the question one player asks in a review: "I am a little over half way there after 40 hours of pure recon gameplay and they're going to wipe on October 15th" (Steam review, October 1, 2026).

The arithmetic: at a little over half, say 55 percent, after 40 hours, the rest takes about 33 more hours. At 2 hours a day that is 17 play days; at 4 hours a day, 9.

Put in your own numbers with the calculator: either the XP you have and need, or the hours played and how far along you are. It tells you if you make it, and how many hours a day you would need if not.
