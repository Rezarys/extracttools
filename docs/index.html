Will you reach your unlock before the wipe?

An unofficial calculator for WARDOGS players. Players report Season 2 and a progression wipe for October 15, 2026. Enter your own numbers and see if your next unlock fits before it, and how many hours a day it would take.

From your own pace

For the case "I am a little over halfway after 40 hours": enter the hours you have put toward the unlock and how far along you are. This assumes the same pace to the end; later levels usually take longer.

Where the default numbers come from

  • 5,000 XP per match: a Steam review of October 2, 2026 ("Estimated at 5000xp per round"). Reported by a player, not official.
  • About an hour per match: a Steam review of October 1, 2026 ("an entire 1-hour match worth of money"). Reported by a player, not official.
  • The October 15 date: see the wipe rules page and its sources.

Each default comes from a single player. Your own numbers always beat them. The calculator runs in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored.
