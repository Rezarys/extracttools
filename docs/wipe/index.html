Season 2 and the expected October 15 wipe: what players reported

Updated on October 15, 2026. Season 2 went live on October 15, 2026, after a maintenance window announced for 08:00 to 09:30 UTC, according to the developer's announcements on the game's Steam news page. No date for a next wipe or a next season had been announced at the time of this update.

The official Season 2 patch notes we read do not mention a wipe of everyone's progression. The only reset we found in official announcements is older: update 0.1.2 says players caught abusing cash and XP exploits, or Real Money Trading, had their cash and progression reset. So we cannot confirm from an official source that a general wipe happened: check your own progression in game.

The rest of this page was written on October 3, 2026 from player reviews, before Season 2. It is what players expected then, not official.

Not confirmed here

  • Whether everyone's progression and unlocks were reset on October 15. Players expected it; the official patch notes we read do not say it.
  • Whether cash was converted into Gold Bars, and at what rate. Press summaries said it would be; we have not checked this against an official source.
  • What would be kept in a wipe. Press summaries said Gold Bars and cosmetics are kept; not checked either.
  • When the next wipe or season comes, and how often wipes will happen. Players write different things, so we do not guess.
